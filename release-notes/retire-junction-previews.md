## What changes

Remove the experimental junction destination boards, road schematics and automatic street-level photo guidance, including their acquisition pipeline and settings. Maneuver instructions, route shields, exit labels and lane arrows remain in the green navigation panel. The ordinary street-level imagery viewer remains available.

Keep geometry-based maneuver timing, initially usable versus preferred lane evidence, conservative OSM lane enrichment, reroute handling and attribution.

## Who is affected

Users navigating with the additional junction previews enabled will see the maneuver panel and map without the extra junction card.

## Required action

No manual action or data migration is required. The removed preview preferences cease to have an effect. Follow the remaining maneuver instructions and lane arrows; ordinary imagery browsing is unaffected.
