## Problem and chosen approach

Accepted ControlChildReservation fixes the entire operation fingerprint at root admission; its late-child binder changes identity/time only. Protocol5.4 instead puts actual final outcome, guard sequence and verified owner receipt digest into the future DM settlement body. Those facts do not exist during ownership-only prepare. Normal forward child normalization also cannot admit a new settlement leaf after the original forward deadline, although5.4 explicitly permits containment/settlement controls then.

Considered: late mutable settlement body/field recipe would broaden the root-fixed-body invariant; predicting or enumerating outcome/digest is impossible; an unauthenticated reference would not prove settlement. Chosen: keep the COMPLETE wire operation root-fixed and reference one preallocated authenticated settlement commitment. The actual fixed owner producer publishes that commitment once after verifying final facts. Add only a purpose-bound recovery-control binder/adopter for the existing configuration-fence-settle edge, never generic deadline relaxation or another forward grant. All other reservations and old signed records remain exact.
