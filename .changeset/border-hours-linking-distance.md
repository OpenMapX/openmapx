A merged border crossing finds the opening hours another agency states up to
OpenConditions' linking distance of 2 km away, in its detail as in a search, and
the extra read of the agencies' own records is made only when a merged crossing
lacks hours. The data-flow disclosures state the areas the vehicle and border
reads send: about 10 km around a vehicle being opened, and the search area
widened by 2 km (or about 4 km around an opened crossing) for those hours.
