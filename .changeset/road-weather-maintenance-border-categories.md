Add "Road weather", "Road maintenance" and "Border crossings" map categories.
Road weather stations are coloured by the most hazardous surface they measure
(dry, wet, snow or slush, ice or frost), listed with their surface and road and
air temperatures, and open to each surface sensor's state, temperatures, grip,
water, snow, ice and salt and the weather above the road. Maintenance vehicles
are coloured by their work (ploughing, salting or sanding, other) and open to
their tasks, plough, spreader and heading. Border crossings are coloured by the
cars' longest current wait, list each direction's wait, sort by wait, and open
to every queue's wait and lanes open per direction with the crossing's status
and opening hours. Each has a filter, a dimmed state for stale or closed
items, and source credits; data section icons gain `thermostat` and
`construction`.
