Replace scalar licence display fields with captured, scoped licensing metadata.
Standard identifiers and expressions derive consistent labels and links; custom
terms carry their own definitions, notices and conditions. Independent software,
data, imagery, style and service grants remain separate through provider results,
photos, native transport, map credits and legal details.

Community extension templates demonstrate the same contributor and licensing
contract. Existing scalar attribution formats and the old licence resolver are
removed; consumers must provide canonical licensing metadata.
