Community service manifests must declare `container.memory` as a literal limit such as `512m` or `1g`; a Compose variable reference is rejected because community values are never interpolated. The service scaffold generated by `openmapx-ext` now includes a memory limit. The renderer also gives every community service a process limit and `no-new-privileges`.
