Data-source results can carry a `track`, where a moving item has just been,
which the map draws as a thin, faint line in the marker's colour under the
markers while the category is active. Road maintenance vehicles draw their
trail with it, sanding joins salting in the orange group with its own
"Sand spreader" name and activity row, and the panel names each activity.
A road weather station with a suspected or confirmed fault notes it in the
list and atop its panel, beside the values it still shows.
