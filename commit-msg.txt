refactor: extract transit, poi-search, and data-source domains into self-contained integrations

Move the remaining domain-specific orchestration logic, types, and API routes out of apps/api/src/ and packages/core/ into three new self-contained orchestrator integrations, continuing the pattern established by the geocoding/weather/photos/routing extraction.

New orchestrator integrations:
- integrations/transit/ — owns TransitProvider, all transit types (Departure, TransitStop, TransitRoute, VehiclePosition, TripPlan, ServiceAlert, …), the stop/route/departure/vehicle orchestrator, dedup, health-circuit-breaker, place-transit lookup, and all transit API routes
- integrations/poi-search/ — owns PoiSearchProvider, CategoryDefinition, CategoryPlace, CategorySearchResponse, CATEGORY_DEFINITIONS, categoryPlaceToPlace, HOURS_FILTER_CATEGORY_IDS, and the /api/category-search route
- integrations/data-source/ — owns DataSourceProvider, DataSourceMeta, DataSourceResult, DataSourceDetail, DataSourceFilterDef, DataSourceAttribution, and the /api/data-sources route

Type ownership migration:
- Move transit types out of packages/core/src/types/transit.ts → integrations/transit/types.ts
- Move category/POI types out of packages/core/src/types/category.ts → integrations/poi-search/types.ts
- Move data-source types out of packages/core/src/types/dataSource.ts → integrations/data-source/types.ts
- Move DirectionsResult, Route, RouteLeg, RouteStep, Waypoint, TravelMode out of packages/core/src/types/directions.ts → integrations/routing/types.ts (already its orchestrator)
- Move IsochroneResult and related types out of packages/core/src/types/isochrone.ts → integrations/routing/types.ts
- Move SearchResult, AutocompleteResult, ReverseGeocodingResult out of packages/core/src/types/search.ts → integrations/geocoding/types.ts
- Move RadarFrame, RadarMeta, WeatherSubLayer, TemperatureUnit, WindSpeedUnit out of packages/core/src/types/weather.ts → integrations/weather/types.ts
- Delete packages/core/src/types/{transit,category,dataSource,directions,isochrone,search,weather}.ts

Route migration:
- Transit routes (/api/transit/stops, /departures, /arrivals, /routes, /vehicles, /alerts, /meta, /place, /trips) now served via ctx.registerRoute() from integrations/transit/
- Category search route (/api/category-search) now served from integrations/poi-search/
- Data sources route (/api/data-sources) now served from integrations/data-source/
- Remove corresponding imports and server.register() calls from apps/api/src/server.ts

integration-host.ts cleanup:
- Remove transit orchestrator bridging (transitOrchestrator.register/unregister on load/unload)
- Remove getTransitProviderAttribution() (now owned by the transit integration)
- Remove providerHealth.reset() on integration.unloaded event
- Remove unused _customHealthCheck local variable

Domain interface cleanup in packages/core/src/domains/:
- Delete domains/transit.ts (TransitProvider, TransitProviderCapabilities, TripPlanParams, …)
- Delete domains/poi-search.ts (PoiSearchProvider, PoiSearchResult)
- Delete domains/data-source.ts (DataSourceProvider)
- Remove their exports from domains/index.ts
- Update map-overlay.ts to import DataSourceDetailSection from @integrations/data-source/types

packages/core re-export update:
- index.ts now re-exports all domain types from their integration packages so consumers (@openmapx/core imports) continue to work without source changes across the app
- Delete 7 internal type files; replace with forwarding exports from integration paths

Test migration:
- Move services/transit/__tests__/{cache,dedup,health,orchestrator,place-transit}.test.ts into integrations/transit/
- Move routes/__tests__/transit.test.ts and data-sources.test.ts into their integrations
- Remove transit test includes from apps/api/vitest.config.ts

Integration manifest updates:
- Add integrations field to bike-sharing, car-sharing, ev-charging, fuel, parking, poi-overpass, scooter-sharing manifests
- Update transit-* manifests to declare dependency on integrations/transit/ orchestrator
